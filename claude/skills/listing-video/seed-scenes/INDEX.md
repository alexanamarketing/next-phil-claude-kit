# Starter scenes

These are the seven starting scenes. The first time you make a listing video, Claude
copies them into your own scenes folder, and from then on they are yours to rename and
tune. Each one names a plain camera move; the look line and the keep-the-room-exactly-
as-it-is list ride along automatically.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Kitchen, slide across past the island | Kitchen | slide across | kitchen.md |
