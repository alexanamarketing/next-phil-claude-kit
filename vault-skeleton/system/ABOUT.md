# About Me

This file is the most important one you will fill out. It answers the questions a new AI session would need to know to work with you effectively. The more honestly and specifically you answer, the less time you spend re-explaining yourself every session.

Fill out each section below. Replace the prompts with your actual answers. Be specific: "I am a solo consultant doing product strategy" is more useful than "I work in tech."

---

## Who I Am and My Role

Prompt: What do you do? What is your professional role, and what kind of work do you primarily use AI for?

Example: I'm a solo marketing consultant. I use AI primarily for client strategy, content drafts, and managing project workflows.

[Your answer here]

## My Expertise Level

Prompt: How technical are you? What should the AI assume you already know, and where should it slow down and explain?

Example: Comfortable with code at a high level but not a developer — explain what a change does, skip syntax basics. Slow down on DevOps and infrastructure.

[Your answer here]

## How I Want You to Communicate

Prompt: What tone do you prefer? How long should responses be? Are there phrases, formats, or styles you want avoided?

Example: Direct and concise. No filler. Skip bullet summaries when a sentence works. Never use em dashes or AI clichés like "leverage" or "robust."

[Your answer here]

## My Do's

Prompt: What behaviors do you actively want? Things like "always ask before saving files", "challenge my assumptions", "give me options when uncertain", or "run tests before calling something done."

Example: Always ask before saving anything to memory. Challenge my reasoning if something seems off. Show options when uncertain rather than picking one silently.

[Your answer here]

## My Absolute Don'ts

Prompt: What should the AI never do when working with you? These are hard lines: writing styles to avoid, actions that require explicit permission, patterns that slow you down.

Example: Never commit or send anything without explicit sign-off. Never write in passive voice. Never add tasks to any system other than todo.md.

[Your answer here]

## How I Like Tasks Tracked

Prompt: Where do your todos live? How do you want them formatted? Any conventions you follow (priority tags, date stamps, etc.)?

Example: Tasks live in each project's todo.md. Use the nested checklist format with {priority:high} and {added:YYYY-MM-DD} tags. Never add flat items that belong under an existing initiative.

## Anything Else a New Assistant Should Know

Prompt: What did you wish an AI assistant knew about you from session one? Quirks, hard-won preferences, context that does not fit anywhere else above.

Example: I work in short bursts — assume context was lost since last time and give me a quick re-orientation. I prefer to approve all final outputs before anything is sent externally.
