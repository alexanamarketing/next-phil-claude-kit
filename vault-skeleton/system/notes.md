### Purpose of this project

`system` is the meta project that holds your identity and workflow knowledge. It is not a client or deliverable project. Its job is to make every future session faster by giving the AI a stable picture of who you are and how you work.

The most important file here is `ABOUT.md`. Filling it out before starting project work is the difference between an AI that re-establishes context each session and one that already knows how to work with you.

- Update ABOUT.md whenever your role, stack, or preferences change materially
